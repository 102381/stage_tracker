# Stage hours

A small page for keeping track of internship hours. You pick your internship (the second one opens on 1 Feb 2027), set your first day, enter the hours you already have, then add each day you work. It shows when you will finish compared to the last day and how far you are from the required hours (620 for the first, 424 for the second).

Live: https://102381.github.io/stage_tracker/

Everything is stored in your own browser and nothing is sent anywhere. To move to another phone or laptop, use Backup in Settings.

On a phone you can add it to the home screen. It also works without a connection after the first visit.

To run it locally, open `index.html`. There is no build step. The required hours and the dates of both internships are set near the top of `app.js`.
